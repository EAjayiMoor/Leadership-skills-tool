# 1 Plan

## Purpose

Decide what problem the first release solves, for whom, and how success will be recognised. Use the source PRD as evidence, while validating its proposed requirements with assessment and commercial owners.

## Required work

- Observe the current assessment process and inventory sample survey, briefing, interview, heatmap and report artefacts.
- Identify the primary user, buyer, decision maker and people affected by the output.
- Choose whether the first proof replays a completed assessment or supports a new one.
- Define the smallest complete workflow and explicit exclusions.
- Baseline manual time, rework and report quality; set target measures.
- Name product, assessment-method, technical, governance and commercial decision owners.
- List risks and dependencies in `docs/decision-log.md`.

## Exit evidence

- Product brief and first-slice hypothesis in `docs/product-foundation.md` are reviewed.
- D-01, D-02, D-03, D-09 and D-10 have named owners and decisions.
- Build-ready sample artefacts are inventoried and approved for prototype use.
- Success measures have a baseline or a plan to measure it.

## AI use

AI may summarise observations, compare options and draft plans. A human product owner confirms the problem, scope and success thresholds.
