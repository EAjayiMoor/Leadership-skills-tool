# Manual workflow context

Status: observational context from a thematic workbook and completed individual reports supplied by the product owner on 24 October 2026. These source files contain identifiable and individual-level assessment information and remain outside this public repository. They help us understand the manual process; they are not approved product requirements, import templates or calculation rules.

## Process visible in the examples

1. Assessors assemble role context and survey questions and answers under recurring themes.
2. They prepare interview focus areas and tailored questions, then bring interview material into the completed record.
3. They interpret the collected evidence, identify themes across the group, and prepare possible actions in a separate thematic working view.
4. The working material feeds human-reviewed reporting. Restricted individual evidence and client-facing thematic conclusions need different access and publication controls.

The reports vary in structure and level of detail. This is useful evidence for discovery, but it does not establish which file formats the first product must ingest or exactly how a future interface should represent each step.

## Product questions these examples help us investigate

- Where does the greatest manual effort occur: gathering inputs, preparing interviews, consolidating evidence, or preparing the final report?
- Which source passages must remain traceable when an assessor writes a cohort finding?
- Which parts of the workflow should the first product slice support, and which can remain manual initially?
- What assessment and heatmap rules do the subject-matter owners want the product to apply?
- What may each user role see, edit, approve and export?

Resolve these with users and owners in Plan and Design. Do not infer a scoring model, permissions matrix or build scope from this example set alone.
