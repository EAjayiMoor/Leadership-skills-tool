# Product foundation

## Working proposition

Help assessment consultants run a tailored leadership assessment with less manual preparation and consolidation while preserving control over evidence, interpretation and publication.

## Initial user and workflow hypothesis

The first user is an assessment consultant or lead. They configure an engagement, import survey and interview material, prepare tailored interview briefs, consolidate evidence into cohort themes, and publish a reviewed report. Client self-service is a separate hypothesis to validate.

The supplied [manual-workflow context](manual-workflow-observations.md) shows how completed individual records and a thematic working view are used today. It informs discovery without deciding the first product slice.

## First product slice

One engagement using approved synthetic or anonymised sample data:

1. Configure themes, participant groups and question sets without code changes.
2. Import agreed sample survey and interview files with clear validation messages.
3. Assemble and edit an interview briefing pack.
4. Attach source evidence to consultant-authored cohort findings.
5. Produce a heatmap only after the method is agreed.
6. Review and export a report from approved findings.

## Outcomes to baseline and measure

- Time to configure an engagement and prepare each interview brief.
- Time from completed evidence collection to reviewed thematic findings.
- Share of published findings with traceable supporting evidence.
- Corrections needed before a client-ready report.
- Ability to configure a second assessment without developer intervention.

## Known boundaries

The reference engagement involved 12 leaders over roughly six to seven weeks. This is one source of workflow evidence, not proof of general demand or a target operating model. The PRD does not establish prices, user volumes, a production hosting choice, an assessment scoring method, or formal commercial targets.
